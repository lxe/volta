## Hardware probes (bench/v100-probe.cu, bench/v100-gemv-lowbit.cu)
HBM read 868 GB/s; FP16 GEMM (cuBLAS, FP32 acc) 100 TFLOPS at M=8192, 40 at 128; launch 1.8 µs (1.24 in a graph);
P2P 48.5 GB/s double-link, 24.3 single-link, ~10 no-link, ~8 µs small copy; H2D pinned 12.4 GB/s.
NCCL 2.25 at 64 KB: all-gather 38 µs, all-reduce 56 µs, reduce-scatter 59 µs.
Low-bit GEMV (magic-number dequant, CUDA cores): 4-bit 746/638/465/305 GB/s at batch 1/2/4/8; 2-bit 627/383/212/124.
