## Existing floating-point ordering limits

volta.rs:1566–1619 calls vmm_z with det=false for the head. For the HP16160x5120head at t8, even_kc selects1024, yielding five K partitions that accumulate through float atomicAdd in kernels/volta.cuh:204. vmm_h also uses split-K atomics for shared-expert projections. Routed expert accumulation and atomic-built worklists provide other possible scheduling-sensitive operations. Floating-point addition is not associative, so differing arrival order can change bits and potentially later routing/argmax. These are source-established mechanisms, not measured causes of the observed KL change. HP mm_det and router zst paths explicitly use fixed-order partial sums; integer last-block tickets alone do not imply variable float order. Do not label every atomic nondeterministic or infer the size of an error from their presence.
