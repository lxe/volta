 How it works:
 - Geometry: square pyramid (apex + 4 base vertices) spun about Y at
   1.15 rad/s, with a slow tilt 0.30 + 0.26*sin(t*0.35) that reveals
   the top and under-side as it spins.
 - Raster: each frame the terminal is re-read via
   shutil.get_terminal_size(), the scene is projected at dot
   resolution (2×4 dots per cell, aspect kept square) and filled with
   an edge-function triangle rasteriser plus a depth buffer so nearer
   faces occlude farther ones.
 - Shading: per face, brightness = 0.10 + 0.55*lambert + 0.55*facing
   (facing = normal·view), mapped onto a 9-step 256-colour grey-blue
   ramp; back faces are culled so faces fade as they turn edge-on.
 - Output: braille bitmask per cell, only changed cells are repainted
   (cursor-addressed escapes), cursor hidden with ESC[?25l and
   restored on SIGINT/SIGTERM/exit.
 - Timing: fixed 30 fps pacing (~4 ms/frame of CPU for an 70×29
   pane), resizes are detected and trigger a full clear.

 Ctrl-C was verified to restore the cursor and return to the shell
 prompt; the animation is left running.
