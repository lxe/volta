## Decision

Reject VOLTA_MEGA=1 for this optimization window. The available V100 results show regressions, and current source retains the measured causes: low persistent occupancy, prefetching dense variants, and disabled overlap. The saved launches do not remove intermediate global-memory traffic or attention/weight arithmetic. An uncapped table cache also adds an unresolved resource bound against the192MiB reserve. No positive current-shape measurement or new mechanism supports reversing those results. There is no proposed enablement, build, or GPU screen; a future reconsideration requires a specific source change that removes a measured cause and a separately budgeted correctness/timing experiment. No gain is booked.
